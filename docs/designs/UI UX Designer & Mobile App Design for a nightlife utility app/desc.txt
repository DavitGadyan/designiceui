UI UX Designer & Mobile App Design for a nightlife utility app
My role. UI UX Mobile App Designer | SaaS Design | Mobile App Development | Mobile App Design | Mobile Design

Project description.

I’ve been working on the UX/UI design for the first product of a U.S.-focused startup building nightlife utility apps.

The app uses live foot-traffic data to show which venues are busiest through a real-time map, rankings, and insights, helping users aged 18–30 decide where to go out.

I designed an iOS-first mobile experience and custom design system, balancing clarity with a sharp, playful tone, while supporting monetization via ads and premium analytics.
