Omni – SaaS Dashboard UI/UX Design for Team & Productivity Management 🧩


As a UI/UX Designer, I designed a modern, intuitive SaaS dashboard for Omni, a team and productivity management platform. The goal was to simplify complex workflows—such as scheduling, team structure, and real-time performance tracking—into a clean, efficient User Interface Design that feels effortless to use.

➡️ Process:

UX/UI Strategy & UI Research:
I began with deep UX Research and UI audits to understand user needs and map an effective User Flow. This included wireframing, usability testing, and crafting a clear information architecture to optimize daily team operations.

UI Design & User Experience:
The final UX/UI Design integrates meeting scheduling, productivity insights, and team visibility into a single dashboard. Using a minimal, dark-mode–friendly aesthetic with strategic color accents, the interface remains focused, modern, and distraction-free.

Responsive & Mobile-Friendly:
Designed with Responsive Design in mind, the platform delivers consistent, high-performing experiences across desktop, tablet, and Mobile Design environments—crucial for remote and on-the-go teams.

🎯 Results:
The result is a seamless User Experience Design that empowers teams to stay aligned and productive. The design elevates Omni’s SaaS product, making it more intuitive, efficient, and scalable.
