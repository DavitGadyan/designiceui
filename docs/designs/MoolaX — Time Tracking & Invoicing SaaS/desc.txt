Project description.

MoolaX is a SaaS time-tracking and invoicing platform built for boutique creative agencies and freelancers. As the sole designer and product owner, I designed and shipped the full product — including onboarding flows, timesheet management, invoice generation, cash flow tracking, bank sync via Plaid, Stripe payment processing, and a team management system. The platform includes a React frontend, Deno/TypeScript backend, and integrations with Plaid, Stripe, and Resend. Currently live at moolax.ai with real users acquired via Google Ads.
