Project description.


For Earnven, I developed a powerful and modern brand identity. The focus was on creating a cohesive visual system that stands out in a competitive market while ensuring consistency across all touchpoints.

Identity Highlights:
✔ Logo Design: A dynamic, angular emblem symbolizing growth, movement, and digital transformation.
✔ Color Palette: A striking combination of deep black and vibrant orange, conveying trust, power, and innovation.
✔ Brand Guide: A comprehensive brand book detailing logo usage, color applications, typography rules, and visual elements for a strong, recognizable presence.
