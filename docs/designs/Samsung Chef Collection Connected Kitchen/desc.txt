Samsung Chef Collection Connected Kitchen
My role. Lead Product Designer

Project description.

I was the lead and only designer for a zero-to-one Samsung Chef Collection concept that paired premium appliances with a dedicated connected-cooking tablet. I helped define the product strategy and owned the content structure, interaction model, tablet UX/UI, and prototypes. The experience moved from cuisine, ingredient, and cooking-time preferences into curated recipes, guided preparation, nutrition, appliance connection, and ingredient fulfillment.
