SaaS Dashboard Design — Complex Data Products
My role. Lead UI/UX Designer & Full Stack Developer

Project description.

Dashboard design for SaaS products that handle complex data — analytics platforms, workflow tools, CRMs, and operations software.

We design for clarity first. Dense data needs hierarchy, not decoration. Our dashboards are built in Figma and handed off dev-ready, or we build them ourselves in React.

Past work includes Interlock (workflow automation SaaS), AI-powered analytics platforms, and multi-tenant B2B tools with role-based views.

For founders whose users are spending hours inside the product — the UX has to work hard.
