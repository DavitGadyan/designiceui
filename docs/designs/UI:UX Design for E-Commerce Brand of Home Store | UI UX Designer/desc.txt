HABIO – UI/UX Design for a Minimalist Home Office E-Commerce Brand 🛋️🛒


As the UI/UX Designer for HABIO, I crafted a bold, editorial-style e-commerce experience for a brand focused on home office aesthetics. The goal was to go beyond the standard online store and build an immersive digital space that feels like a premium design magazine—curated, minimal, and visually striking.

➡️ Process:

UI/UX Research & Strategy:
We started with audience insights and competitive benchmarks in the lifestyle and interior space. Through moodboards, wireframes, and flow mapping, we shaped a User Experience rooted in simplicity and visual clarity. The aim: make browsing feel like flipping through a high-end design editorial.

User Interface Design:
The design centers around a clean, high-contrast layout with generous white space, soft shadows, and elegant product staging. Extra-bold headlines dominate the screen, establishing a confident tone. Strategic use of pink adds character and functions as a visual cue for interaction, creating a balance between aesthetics and usability.

Minimal Navigation & Seamless Flow:
Navigation was intentionally stripped down—discreet when not needed, but instantly accessible when users begin exploring. Each interaction is purposeful and supports the brand’s minimalist philosophy.

Mobile-Optimized & Fully Responsive:
From mobile to desktop, the UI adapts gracefully, with responsive layouts and touch-friendly interactions. Mobile UX was fine-tuned to feel just as editorial, clean, and engaging as the desktop experience.

🎯 Results:
The final design positions HABIO as more than an e-commerce platform—it’s a lifestyle destination. The UI/UX redefines how digital retail can feel in the design-forward home office niche: clean, confident, and curated.
