My role. Senior UI UX Designer, Web designer, Motion Designer

Project description.

Getahead is an on demand mental fitness app for athletes, official mental training partner of HYROX and Ultra X.
What I did. End to end UI UX Designer role: restructured the app around personalized training plans (My Plan, Progress, Support, More), redesigned onboarding with an AI driven mental assessment, and delivered three full UI UX visual identity directions so the client chose between systems, not aesthetics. Web design principles applied across mobile UI for cross device consistency.
Outcome. Coherent UX UI shipped live on iOS and Android.

Outcome

Live on iOS and Android with a defined visual language and four-surface structure that reads as performance gear, not therapy. Official mental training partner of HYROX and Ultra X - the brand earned category positioning a generic mindfulness product can't reach.
Challenge

Two problems to solve. Two challenges to overcome.
- First, the product itself was too feature-centric and not journey-centric enough. Users want a plan to follow and a way to track and manage their progress.
- Second, the visual expression of the brand struggled to land in a tight category gap between premium and high-performance, yet calm, and structured but not SaaS, and intentional but not a journaling app.
Approach

- IA & user flows. We moved towards outlining the app around mental training plans instead of individual training sessions. We defined four core areas of the app and the user journeys along those areas, including onboarding through taking a mental assessment, generating a plan, daily journeys through the app, audio playback and journaling.
- Onboarding redesign: Turning sign-up into the first value delivered to the customer. An AI-powered assessment is given to the user and they receive a highly personal initial plan as a result.
- Visual Identity Exploration. Of these explorations three truly unique design systems were developed; not variations on a theme, but each a complete solution to the initial brand brief.
- The new visual identity for Performance Energy features structured panels, high contrast colours and electric accents on a deep black background.
- Muted / Mindful - typography led hierarchy, almost no boxes and a lots of space.
- Abstract / Atmospheric - full-bleed gradients, glass overlays, glowing progress indicators.

Each direction included Plan Overview and Profile Overview screens with full typography, color logic, button language, progress treatment and tone - so the client chose between systems, not aesthetics.
UI design. Designed the core screens in Figma (onboarding, My Plan, session overview, audio session, journal) and produced a style guide for extending the system across the product.
