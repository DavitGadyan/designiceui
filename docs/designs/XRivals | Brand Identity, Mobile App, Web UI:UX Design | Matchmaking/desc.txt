XRivals | Brand Identity, Mobile App, Web UI/UX Design | Matchmaking
My role. Lead UI/UX Designer

Project description.

I designed the brand identity, mobile app, and web experience for Xrivals, a platform that helps users find players to match with in tennis, padel, and squash, and book courts at nearby venues. My work covered UX flows, wireframes, and responsive UI for player matchmaking, social features, and booking management. The design emphasizes usability, clean navigation, and conversion for venue partners. Deliverables: brand identity system, UX research, mobile app UI, website UX, design system, prototyping, product strategy.
