Ballers Bridge: Basketball Recruiting Platform
My role. Co-founder and head of product and marketing design.

Project description.

Ballers Bridge is a basketball recruiting and highlight platform for high-school players, coaches, and scouts. I designed the original player-facing experience, including a marketing site, player profile, and dashboard for uploading and sharing game film. For this portfolio presentation, I refreshed the player profile and dashboard to clarify the recruiting journey and bring the dark, basketball-first visual system into a contemporary product-design language.
