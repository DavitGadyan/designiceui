Project description.

QuantumFit was a habit mobile application project where we created the brand identity, website, and product experience. The work combined UX/UI design, branding, and digital product strategy to deliver a sleek, modern interface for a mobile app. The app was designed to help users track progress, stay engaged, and connect with personalized training.
