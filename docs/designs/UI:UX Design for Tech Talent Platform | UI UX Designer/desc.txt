Extendix – UI/UX Design for an On-Demand Tech Talent Platform 👩‍💻⚡


As the UI/UX Designer for Extendix, I crafted a confident, streamlined platform that helps product teams instantly connect with pre-vetted tech specialists. Unlike generic talent pools, Extendix highlights real people—ready to integrate directly into growing teams with speed, trust, and transparency.

➡️ Process

UI/UX Research & Strategy:
We began by analyzing recruitment platforms and gathering insights from fast-scaling product companies. The core challenge was to balance clarity with speed: making talent discovery frictionless while maintaining a professional, trust-building feel. Wireframes and user flows were developed to prioritize transparency and fast decision-making.

User Interface Design:
The design focuses on people, not just profiles. Individual specialists are featured with availability indicators and role highlights, allowing teams to quickly assess fit. A confident visual style was chosen: bold typography, clean grids, and sharp contrasts emphasize clarity, while subtle orange accents guide attention to critical actions.

Content & Flow:
From the homepage, users are met with a strong positioning message and a streamlined browsing experience. The flow naturally guides them from talent discovery to selection, reducing noise and keeping interactions direct and purposeful.

Responsive & Mobile-Optimized:
Built with a mobile-first mindset, the interface adapts across devices, ensuring managers can discover and hire talent seamlessly—whether at their desk or on the move.

🎯 Results:
The final design positions Extendix as more than a recruitment platform—it’s a bridge between growing product teams and trusted specialists. By highlighting real people and real availability, Extendix sets a new benchmark in talent transparency and speed.
