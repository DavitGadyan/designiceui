Social Network for Pilots

Project description.

OVERVIEW
Design a mobile application for a social network for pilots

ROLE
Lead Product Designer

GOAL & CHALLENGE
The goal was to design a mobile app from scratch, adding a social twist to familiar logbook management. The key challenge was to strike a balance between pilots focused on logging complex technical records and those keen to casually share their flight stories, all the while empowering both to engage within the community.

SOLUTION
My approach involved creating a multifaceted experience where a social element served as a unifying space for all user types.

OVERVIEW
Design a mobile application for a social network for pilots.

ROLE
Lead Product Designer

GOAL & CHALLENGE
The goal was to design a mobile app from scratch, adding a social twist to familiar logbook management. The key challenge was to strike a balance between pilots focused on logging complex technical records and those keen to casually share their flight stories, all the while empowering both to engage within the community.

SOLUTION
My approach involved creating a multifaceted experience where a social element served as a unifying space for all user types. Although complex technical features did not occupy a prominent spot, they were easily accessible for power users as needed. The tracking mode, designed as an effective in-flight assistant, proved successful in driving engagement.

- User Research

- Information Architecture
