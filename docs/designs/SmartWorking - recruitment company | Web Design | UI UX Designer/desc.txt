SmartWorking - company specializing in recruitment for remote work, helping businesses connect with top talent for remote positions.


Design Approach:
The goal was to create a custom design that enhanced navigation, readability, and visual appeal, while focusing on an intuitive user experience. We streamlined the layout to ensure easy access to key information and an effortless flow for users browsing job opportunities or seeking talent.

Result:
As UI UX Designer I delivered a modern, visually appealing design that highlighted SmartWorking’s innovative approach to remote work recruitment. The enhanced user interface made it easy for job seekers and companies to navigate, improving engagement and making the recruitment process more efficient. The clean, user-friendly layout aligned with SmartWorking’s forward-thinking values and helped establish a strong online presence.

Process:
Client Consultation & Requirements Gathering: Worked closely with the client to understand their goals, target audience, and design preferences.
Wireframing & User Journey Mapping: Developed wireframes to plan the layout and user flow for easy navigation.
Design Phase: Focused on a modern aesthetic with clean typography, intuitive buttons, and effective use of color to guide users.
Responsive Development: Ensured the site is mobile-friendly and fully functional across all devices for a seamless experience.
Testing & Refinement: Conducted thorough usability testing to address any issues with navigation and ensure the website met the client's expectations.
