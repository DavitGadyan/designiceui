Cyber Ronin is a high-aggression dark landing page: neon noir, hard edges, and type
that behaves like a HUD.

Hero. A slowly rotating low-poly 3D object - a faceted blade or mask - lit by two coloured rim
lights, magenta #FF2E63 from the left and cyan #00F0FF from the right, on near-black. Rendered
with Three.js, ACES filmic tone mapping applied so the model does not render dark and muddy, an
environment map at low intensity, and no ambient light beyond a 0.15 hemisphere.

Type. Condensed uppercase display with heavy tracking on the eyebrow and none on the headline. A
1-2px RGB split on hover for the primary CTA, plus a 40ms glitch displacement fired every 6-9
seconds at random on the headline so it never feels scheduled.

Scan lines. A repeating linear-gradient overlay at 3% opacity, 3px period, plus a horizontal
sweep bar that crosses the viewport every 11 seconds.

Feature blocks. Hard-cornered panels, no radius, 1px #FF2E6344 borders, corner ticks drawn as
small L shapes at each corner. Numbered 01 through 04 in mono.

Palette is black with exactly two neons. They never blend into a gradient; they stay separate and
always sit on black. Restricting to two is what keeps it menacing instead of chaotic.

Respect prefers-reduced-motion by dropping the glitch and the sweep and holding the 3D object
still - this style is the most likely of any to cause discomfort.

Use for cybersecurity, gaming, esports, crypto and hardware with an edge.
