Project description.

I worked on the UX/UI design for a Bangkok-based startup building a peer-to-peer web app for renting and lending fashion.

The goal was to make fashion rental simple, stylish, and trustworthy while preparing the product for launch. I designed a mobile-first, responsive experience with item listings, availability calendars, filters, KYC verification, reviews, messaging, and delivery tracking.

Inspired by the refined taste I shaped a clean, urban-chic interface that supports confident styling and drives commission-based bookings in Thailand.
