Barrett-Jackson Website Redesign for a High-Stakes Auction Brand
My role. Lead UX and Design Partner

Project description.

Barrett-Jackson’s digital experience no longer matched the clarity, energy, or trust of its live auctions. I led the marketing-site redesign across research, information architecture, content strategy, UX, and UI. I reorganized the site around buyer and seller intent, made vehicles, auction details, and deadlines easier to find, and turned the homepage into a clear entry point for browsing cars, attending auctions, consigning, and bidding. The result was a more modern, premium experience and a stronger foundation for Barrett-Jackson’s core customer journeys.
