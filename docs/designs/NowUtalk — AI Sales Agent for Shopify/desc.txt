My role. Senior Product Designer · Brand Lead

Project description.

NowUtalk is an AI sales expert seamlessly integrated with Shopify to handle the entire sales cycle like a real salesperson. Kayen Peppa crafted a distinctive visual identity combining a clean white canvas with bold black typography and a vibrant violet-to-pink gradient translated into expressive flowing waveforms. The mobile experience was approached as central to the product story, showcasing how the AI sales agent integrates into the Shopify mobile shopping flow through clean conversational interfaces and clearly framed product cards that guide visitors from discovery to checkout.
