My role. Senior UX/UI designer, Lead designer

Project description.

Swoop is a mobile application that on the one hand allows you to order a taxi and on the other hand, earn money if you are a driver. The project is as transparent and easy to use as possible, the bright design makes it recognizable and modern.

The project was divided into several stages:
- Development of prototypes and clickability
- Branding and ui concept
- Visual design of all internal pages of the project for two users - drivers and clients
- Development of marketing materials for the project
