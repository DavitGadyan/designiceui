Heritage Grove is a warm, paper-toned site for a land and sustainability brand. It is
the counterweight to dark tech: cream backgrounds, deep olive text, photography that looks shot
on film.

Hero. Not a full-bleed image. A wide cream field with a serif headline at the optical centre and
a single landscape photograph inset into a rounded-corner frame that extends past the right
margin. The image drifts up at 0.85x scroll speed for a gentle parallax.

Grain. A subtle noise overlay at 4% opacity sits over the whole page, animated at 8fps rather
than 60 so it reads as film grain rather than shimmer. This one detail does most of the work of
making the page feel analogue.

Story timeline. A vertical rule down the left with year markers. Each entry fades and rises as it
enters, and the rule itself draws downward as the section scrolls, scrubbed to scroll position.

Marquee. A slow horizontal ticker of place names in the serif at 96px, moving right to left at
about 25 seconds per loop, clipped by the section edges. Slow enough to be readable, which is what
separates a good marquee from a nervous one.

Footer. Oversized wordmark set as a full-width lockup, cropped by the bottom of the viewport, with
the navigation in small caps above it.

Palette is bone #F4EFE6, deep olive #2F3A2C for text, tan #8A6A43 for accents. No pure black, no
pure white anywhere - that is deliberate and it is why the page feels warm. Type is a
high-contrast literary serif for display with a neutral sans for UI.

Use for sustainability, agriculture, wineries, hospitality, heritage brands and anything where
the story is longer than the product.
