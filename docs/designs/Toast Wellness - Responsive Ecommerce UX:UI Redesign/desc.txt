Toast Wellness - Responsive Ecommerce UX/UI Redesign
My role. Sole UX/UI Designer and Ecommerce Strategist

Project description.

I redesigned Toast Wellness’s existing CBD store into a clearer, more premium ecommerce experience across desktop and mobile. As the sole designer, I reworked the information architecture, navigation, homepage, product discovery, product-detail experience, and purchasing flows. The new direction combined familiar ecommerce patterns with product education, trust signals, variant selection, FAQs, and need-based shopping paths. I also created dark editorial and lighter visual directions. Elements of the work were carried into Toast’s evolved live storefront.
