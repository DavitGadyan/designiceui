Project description.

I designed a next-generation AI-powered Property Platform tailored specifically for the UAE real estate market. The platform brings together three core experiences in one cohesive product.
An intelligent AI assistant guides property discovery through natural conversation, surfacing relevant listings based on preferences, budget and location without the friction of traditional search.
For Admin, users, and agents its a fully featured backend dashboard to oversee accounts, listings, platform activity and data across the entire ecosystem.
