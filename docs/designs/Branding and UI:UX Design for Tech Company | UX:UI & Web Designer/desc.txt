Teknowledge – Global Corporate Website Redesign & Motion-Rich UI 🌐

As the UI/UX Designer for Teknowledge, I executed a full digital redesign and visual transformation for a global technology leader specializing in AI, customer experience, and cybersecurity. The project aimed to turn an outdated corporate website into a motion-driven, engaging platform that communicates innovation, expertise, and scale across enterprise audiences.
The redesign centered on a fluid, abstract logo that reflects the continuous interplay between technology and knowledge, setting the tone for a dynamic digital presence. Story-driven landing pages, interactive 3D elements, and animated components guide users naturally through over 20 pages, blending brand storytelling with clear, intuitive UX.

➡️ Process
Brand & Visual Alignment
Working hand-in-hand with brand strategists, I translated the refreshed Teknowledge identity into a flexible UX/UI system. The goal was to unify visuals, motion, and interface logic so that global teams can apply the brand consistently across 50+ pages and thousands of employees.
UI/UX Design & Motion Integration
Each page was built to communicate expertise while keeping engagement high. Motion graphics, subtle animations, and 3D elements guide attention, add depth, and make interactions feel alive — from the logo’s first appearance to scrolling through dynamic content blocks.
Information Architecture & Scalable Design
Layouts were structured to support enterprise users and decision-makers. Clear visual hierarchy, story-driven content, and modular grids ensure readability and scalability, allowing the platform to grow as the company expands its offerings.
Interactive Templates & Brand Toolkit
To make the brand practical and consistent, I designed templates for presentations, business cards, email signatures, and social graphics. These assets help the 6,000+ employees maintain a coherent brand voice without extra oversight.
Responsive & Global Deployment
The platform was optimized for mobile and desktop across 19+ global hubs. Every interaction, motion, and layout element was tested for clarity, performance, and brand consistency, ensuring a seamless experience worldwide.

🎯 Results
The Teknowledge website evolved from outdated and static to dynamic and motion-rich, presenting a cohesive, globally-scalable digital identity. The project delivered:
• A story-driven corporate website that communicates expertise in AI, cybersecurity, and digital transformation
• Motion and 3D elements that engage enterprise audiences while guiding attention naturally
• A scalable design system and brand toolkit supporting over 6,000 employees worldwide
• Fully responsive layouts optimized for desktop and mobile
• A polished, interactive platform that strengthens global brand presence
Today, the site is a benchmark for enterprise-level, motion-integrated UX/UI — modern, fluid, and built to reflect a global tech leader.
