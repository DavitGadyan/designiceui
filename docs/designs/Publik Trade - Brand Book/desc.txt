The problem addressed in developing the branding and Brand Book for Publik Trade was the need for a distinct identity in a competitive DeFi options trading landscape. As a new entrant, Publik Trade lacked an established presence. The opportunity lay in creating a minimalistic logo and comprehensive Brand Book that not only reflected professionalism but also instilled trust. The challenge was to design an identity that communicated seriousness, aligning with the project's nature, while adhering to the client's color preferences. The resulting branding positions Publik Trade as a credible and robust player in the decentralized finance market, essential for garnering trust and establishing a solid foundation in a highly competitive industry.

The solution for Publik Trade was to design an original and minimalistic logo, with a bold "P" in the center surrounded by a circle - a symbolic representation of options trading. This circle also reflects the decentralized nature of the platform, with a subtle hint of blockchain depicted in the form of a chain. Beyond aesthetics, the design is versatile, allowing for seamless integration into a variety of platforms, from websites to social media to marketing materials. The sleek and animated logo effectively conveys the professionalism and reliability required for Publik Trade to make a name for itself in the competitive DeFi options trading market, creating a strong visual identity in line with the project's goals.
