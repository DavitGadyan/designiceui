Mindora is a wellness landing page built on softness. Every corner is round, every
transition is slow, and nothing on the page moves faster than a breath.

Hero. Centred, single column, max-width 720px. Behind it, an animated mesh gradient in lilac
#E9DFF7, sage #C9E4DE and blush #F2C6B4, implemented as three large blurred radial blobs drifting
on independent 20-30 second sine paths. The blur is heavy - 120px - so the colours never resolve
into distinct shapes.

Breathing element. A circle that scales 1.0 to 1.08 over a 4s inhale, holds 1s, returns over 4s.
It is the page's metronome and every other duration on the site is derived from it: entrances are
600ms, hovers 240ms, so the whole page feels like it shares one pulse.

Typography. A soft variable serif for display, weight 400, generous 1.15 line height on headings
and 1.7 on body. Body copy is set at 18px minimum because the audience reads rather than scans.

Cards. 24px radius, no border, a single very soft shadow 0 8px 32px rgba(59,55,70,0.06). White
cards on the warm off-white ground, separated by shadow alone.

Testimonials. Three quotes in a slow auto-advancing carousel, 7 second dwell, crossfade rather
than slide, paused on hover and on focus.

Palette is warm off-white with three desaturated pastels. Text is a soft near-black #3B3746, never
pure black - pure black against pastel reads as harsh and undoes the whole effect.

Use for meditation, therapy, sleep, fertility, mental health, skincare and gentle consumer apps.
