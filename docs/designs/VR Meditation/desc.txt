Project description.

YourHaven is a meditation app for Meta Quest - guided sessions across imaginary galaxies and tranquil vistas. Live in the Meta Store alongside Tripp, Headspace XR, and Guided Meditation VR.

What I did
- Spatial navigation and session library
- Custom-environment selection flow
- Onboarding into the VR meditation paradigm
- Visual language calibrated for headset immersion
