OVERVIEW
Design a web and mobile application for a workplace safety HR platform.

ROLE
Lead Product Designer

GOAL & CHALLENGE
The goal was to design a web admin panel and client mobile app to assist offices in safely reopening during the pandemic. The primary challenge involved the sensitive task of monitoring employees' health while preserving their privacy. Furthermore, adapting to the constantly evolving health guidelines was also a critical aspect.

SOLUTION
My design approach prioritized user privacy and trust, with a strong emphasis on transparent communication during the onboarding process. A primary focus was the automation of most features, coupled by a robust notification system. Additionally, the apps were designed with modular feature components to ensure flexibility in response to shifting regulations.

INVOLVEMENT
- User Research
- Journey Maps
- Storyboards
- Information Architecture
- Wireframing & Prototyping
- UI/UX Design
- Motion Design
