My role. Senior Product Designer · Brand Lead

Project description.

AJ+ is a global media brand creating bold, visually engaging journalism that brings underreported stories to the forefront. Kayen Peppa was commissioned to redesign their digital experience to reflect the brand's bold, energetic, and socially driven nature. The goal was to create a vibrant visual universe using expressive typography, strong contrast, and layered media content to capture attention and amplify storytelling. The mobile experience was approached as a key part of the platform, preserving the brand's editorial energy while ensuring content remains engaging on smaller screens.
