PAWVER – UI/UX Design for a Smart Pet Tech E-commerce Brand 🐾📱


As the UI/UX Designer for PAWVER, I created a warm, emotionally-driven e-commerce experience for a brand specializing in smart pet technology. The goal was to make advanced tech feel personal and inviting—bridging innovation with the deep emotional bond people share with their pets.

➡️ Process:

UX Research & Emotional Strategy:
Before diving into the interface, I explored user personas, competitive products, and emotional cues in pet ownership. We discovered early that tech wasn’t the hook—connection was. The entire user flow was built to mirror the experience of discovering something delightful for someone you love.

UI Design Approach:
The first visual on the site is a soulful dog making direct eye contact—immediately setting an emotional tone. The UI design follows suit: soft gradients, rounded edges, clean product cards, and a calming scroll rhythm that feels intuitive, not transactional. The palette is gentle and pet-friendly—more “cozy home” than “cold tech.”

Interactive Elements:
From subtle hover states to live product previews, every interaction was designed to feel responsive and "alive," reflecting the smart tech at the core of the product line without overwhelming the user with specs or jargon.

Mobile-First & Responsive:
Mobile interactions were key—users often browse pet products casually. The mobile version was crafted with thumb-friendly navigation, intuitive filtering, and a scroll path that encourages relaxed, playful exploration.

🎯 Results:
The final product is an e-commerce platform that doesn't just sell devices—it builds trust and joy. PAWVER now presents its smart pet tech with emotional clarity and visual softness, helping users connect instantly and shop confidently.
