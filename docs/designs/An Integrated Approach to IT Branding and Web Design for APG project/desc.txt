APG - a new US-based company in California that specializes in distributing and reselling cleaning chemicals, including degreasers, to various municipalities, government agencies, and businesses.

Project Overview:
APG approached us with a need for a complete branding solution, including logo development, product label design, and a website to showcase their range of cleaning products. Their main requirement was to establish a modern and minimalist visual identity, setting them apart from outdated competitors in the niche.

Solution:
I developed a fresh, sleek, and modern brand identity that resonates with both professional and government sectors. The design reflects cleanliness, professionalism, and trustworthiness. We designed a clean, minimal logo, dynamic product labels, and a visually engaging website that emphasizes APG’s high-quality offerings. The site was crafted to showcase their products effectively and promote the brand’s professionalism.

Result:
I helped APG position itself as a recognized leader in the industry, earning the trust of customers through modern, visually appealing branding. The updated design also contributed to a positive and memorable first impression, boosting brand recognition and credibility.

Process:
Branding Development: We started by designing a logo that represents the company’s core values: cleanliness, reliability, and modernity.
Product Label Design: Created clean, straightforward labels that align with the company’s new visual identity.
Website Design: Developed a responsive website with a minimalist design, focusing on clear communication of products and services.
Responsive Web Development: Ensured the site was mobile-friendly and functional on all devices, enhancing user engagement.
Final Testing & Optimization: Performed usability testing to ensure optimal functionality and user experience.
