INNERFORM – Branding & Website Design for an Architectural Studio 🏛️


As the UI/UX Designer for INNERFORM, I translated the studio’s architectural philosophy into a digital experience. The goal wasn’t to decorate — it was to capture their essence: clarity, restraint, and emotional intent. Every interaction, every visual choice had to feel like an extension of their architectural practice.

➡️ Process:

UI/UX Research & Strategy:
We started by analyzing the studio’s positioning in the architecture market and studied how their projects balanced minimalism with emotional resonance. Through moodboards, wireframes, and user flows, we built a digital system that mirrors architectural logic: clear zoning, structured rhythm, and intuitive navigation.

User Interface Design:
Typography became the furniture. Whitespace became light. Motion became rhythm. Each element was deliberately stripped of excess to let the brand’s philosophy breathe. Warm contrasts softened the minimal structure, giving the website a human feel while preserving clarity.

3D Interaction Design:
A central feature — the 3D room selector — was designed not just as a UI component, but as a spatial metaphor. It allows users to explore the studio’s approach in an experiential way, bridging the gap between digital interaction and architectural presence.

Minimal Navigation & Seamless Flow:
The interface was intentionally quiet — guiding, not distracting. Interactions remain subtle yet intentional, ensuring that the content leads, while the design frames the experience.

Mobile-Optimized & Fully Responsive:
The design adapts seamlessly across devices. On mobile, the same principles — rhythm, clarity, and restraint — create an experience that feels architectural, not templated.

🎯 Results:
The final design positions INNERFORM as more than an architectural studio — it transforms their website into a digital reflection of their philosophy. Strong structure, emotional tone, and refined interactions elevate their online presence into something timeless and spatial.
