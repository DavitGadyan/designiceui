Sandbar AI: AML Transaction Monitoring UX
My role. Product Strategist & UX/UI Designer

Project description.

Sandbar.ai is an anti-money-laundering platform for transaction monitoring, suspicious-activity alerts, and case management. I shaped product and content strategy, conducted user research and journey mapping, created wireframes and UX/UI designs, and established a new design system. The work focused on giving teams a scalable, defensible way to review alerts, investigate activity, manage cases, and support safe user-base growth.
