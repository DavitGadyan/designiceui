Project description.

Hairspring is a luxury watch ecommerce experience designed to showcase timeless craftsmanship. Kayen Peppa created a premium digital storefront using refined typography, generous whitespace, cinematic imagery, and a restrained visual system to evoke exclusivity and craftsmanship. The UX and information architecture were refined to balance luxury storytelling with ecommerce usability, ensuring users can browse, discover, and evaluate products without friction while the brand's premium feel is preserved throughout.
