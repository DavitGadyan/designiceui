Shopify Store Re-design

Project description.

Luxury lavender producer Lamiale partnered with Stera Global to revamp their website. Aiming to elevate brand identity and product elegance online, We conducted in-depth discovery, crafted a user-friendly UX, and implemented a visually captivating design. The result? A website that reflects Lamiale's essence and provides a luxurious online experience, allowing customers to seamlessly discover Lamiale's story and exquisite lavender products, fostering deeper brand loyalty.
