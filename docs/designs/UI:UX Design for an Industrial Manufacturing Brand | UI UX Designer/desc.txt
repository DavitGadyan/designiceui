Carbo – Industrial Web Design for a Global Manufacturing Brand ⚙️🌍

As the sole designer, I led the complete digital transformation for Carbo, a global industrial brand with over 45 years of manufacturing expertise. Known for their ultra-durable ceramic beads used in oil, energy, and advanced manufacturing — Carbo needed a digital presence as tough, precise, and forward-looking as their product.

This wasn’t just a redesign. It was a ground-up rebuild of a 50+ page platform that helped take the company from near-bankruptcy to full capacity production — within three months of launch.

➡️ Process:

Brand-to-Web Strategy:
Working from a new rebrand, I translated Carbo’s industrial legacy into a modern, tech-forward web system. Every design decision — from typography to motion — served the goal of clarity, confidence, and functional performance.

Signature Visual Concept – The Bead:
I placed the ceramic bead at the center of the digital experience. On the homepage, a 3D-rendered bead slowly rotates with a subtle red pulse — a visual metaphor for engineering precision and R&D innovation.

UI/UX & Interaction Design:
• Designed 50+ unique pages across product, industry, and content verticals
• Created a dual-path navigation system for users: by product or by use case
• Developed custom search, dynamic content cards, and an intuitive mega menu
• Responsive design optimized across desktop, tablet, and mobile
• Integrated smart forms to qualify leads and protect deep content

Immersive Content Experience:
• Art-directed industrial photography: real operators, real machines, real heat
• Designed and scripted a full launch video with custom 3D elements
• Built a Knowledge Hub: searchable, sortable, with downloadable resources and gated technical documents

🎯 Results:
Carbo sold out all production for the next three months post-launch. The success led to a new plant in planning and the launch of a second venture — all powered by a new digital foundation built by one designer.
