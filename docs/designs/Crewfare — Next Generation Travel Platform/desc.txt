Project description.

Crewfare is on a mission to modernize the way people book travel for live experiences. Kayen Peppa created a design and brand aesthetic that resonates with a younger audience, balancing a fun approach with trust and excitement. The mobile experience was a primary focus given the audience profile. The user journey was redesigned to allow users to quickly and efficiently book hotels, select room types, and check out with split payments across group members — one of the platform's most complex UX challenges.
