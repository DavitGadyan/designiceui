Proterra – UI/UX Design for an Eco-Friendly Sports Nutrition Brand 🌱

As the UI/UX Designer for Proterra, I crafted a clean, inspiring, and functional e-commerce experience for a brand that produces sustainable sports nutrition and supplements. The challenge was to merge performance-driven energy with the brand’s core value — harmony with nature.

➡️ Process:

UI/UX Research & Strategy:
We began by exploring the sports nutrition and eco-lifestyle markets, identifying the balance between performance aesthetics and sustainable branding. Through competitive analysis, moodboards, and user flow mapping, we shaped a shopping experience that feels simple, natural, and motivating.

User Interface Design:
The design highlights the products with bold imagery, clean typography, and a structured grid. Each element was intentionally minimal to let the product and its story shine. A soft, nature-inspired color palette paired with strategic whitespace created a sense of purity and balance.

Storytelling & Brand Values:
Beyond product cards, we developed storytelling blocks that communicate Proterra’s mission — sustainability, performance, and well-being. The visual language connects users emotionally, reinforcing that every purchase supports a healthier lifestyle and a healthier planet.

Navigation & Shopping Flow:
Navigation was designed to be intuitive, leading users from discovery to checkout with minimal friction. Clear categorization, product filters, and seamless cart interactions ensure the user journey is both fast and enjoyable.

Responsive & Mobile-First:
Given the audience’s active lifestyle, mobile optimization was a priority. The UI adapts seamlessly across devices, ensuring a clean, touch-friendly experience that feels as natural on mobile as on desktop.

🎯 Results:
The final design positions Proterra as more than an online store — it’s a brand experience that reflects strength, clarity, and sustainability. The e-commerce platform delivers a seamless journey where health, energy, and nature meet.

💼 Key Highlights:
• Designed a clean, performance-driven UI for an eco-lifestyle brand
• Developed an intuitive UX that simplifies the shopping journey
• Created a storytelling-driven visual language emphasizing sustainability
• Delivered a fully responsive, mobile-optimized e-commerce platform
• Balanced sports aesthetics with natural, eco-friendly tones
