Playful Idea is loud on purpose. Thick black outlines, hard offset shadows, primary
colours, and elements that look like stickers dropped onto the page.

Hero. Cream #FFFDF5 ground. Headline in a chunky display face at clamp(3rem, 8vw, 7rem) with
individual words rotated between -3 and +3 degrees. Behind the type, three flat shapes - a blob, a
star, a squiggle - drift slowly and rotate. A yellow #FFE24B button with a 3px black border and a
6px hard offset shadow that collapses to 2px on press, which is the single most satisfying
interaction on the page.

Cards. 3px #111 borders, 8px hard shadows, no blur. On hover they translate -4px and -4px so the
shadow appears to grow. Rotation of 1-2 degrees at rest keeps the grid from feeling mechanical.

Marquee. Two stacked tickers running in opposite directions, one black on yellow, one yellow on
black, both at 20s per loop, separated by a 4px black rule.

Colour rule. Four colours maximum, each fully saturated, never gradients, never tints. Maximalism
works only when the palette is disciplined; the busyness has to come from composition.

Type is a heavy geometric display with a neutral sans for body, and body copy stays at a
comfortable 17px so the page is still readable under all the noise.

Use for consumer apps, creative agencies, events, education, food brands and anything targeting
a younger audience.
