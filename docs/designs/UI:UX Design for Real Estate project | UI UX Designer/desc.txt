Struktura – UI/UX Design for Frame House Builders 🏡🌲


As the UI/UX Designer for Struktura, I designed a digital experience that goes beyond showcasing architecture—it captures the essence of “home.” The goal was to combine modern aesthetics with natural elements and create a website that feels calm, confident, and inspiring.

➡️ Process

UI/UX Research & Strategy:
We analyzed competitor websites in construction and architecture, focusing on how homeowners make decisions about frame houses. Through moodboards and wireframes, we built a structure that blends clarity with emotional resonance—making the process of exploring housing options feel simple, yet inspiring.

User Interface Design:
The interface draws on clean architectural lines and a nature-inspired palette. Ample white space and forest-toned accents give the site a grounded, calming feel. Motion design and a 3D showreel bring houses to life—helping visitors imagine themselves inside. Typography was chosen to be modern yet warm, balancing professionalism with approachability.

Content & Flow:
The homepage opens with a strong message, then flows seamlessly into benefits, projects, and brand philosophy. Each block was designed to “breathe,” giving content space and maintaining an easy rhythm of exploration.

Responsive & Mobile-Optimized:
The design adapts smoothly across devices. On mobile, navigation remains simple and fluid, with touch-friendly interactions ensuring the same calm, confident experience as desktop.

🎯 Results:
The final design positions Struktura as more than just a construction company—it’s a lifestyle brand for people who want modern, nature-connected living. By blending architecture, UX, and emotion, the site makes frame houses not only look beautiful but feel like home.
