Golfytix: Mobile Golf Training and Performance Platform
My role. Lead Product Designer and Product Strategist

Project description.

Worked directly with the founding team to shape a new golf-training platform for players and instructors. I designed the mobile experience for account setup, instructor matching, 12-week training plans, drills, on-course progress, and skill-specific performance tracking. I also designed the desktop management system for administering users, instructors, content, and the data behind the player experience.
