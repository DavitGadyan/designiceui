Anova Culinary: Ecommerce Product Pages & Brand Storytelling
My role. Design Director and UX Strategy Lead

Project description.

After redesigning Anova Culinary’s recipe experience, my engagement expanded into ecommerce product pages for the Precision Cooker Pro and Precision Oven. Working with the VP of Marketing, I owned UX strategy, content architecture, responsive UX/UI, visual direction, and motion concepts. I structured each page as a progressive story connecting product education with pricing, bundles, comparisons, accessories, recipes, reviews, and community. The selected direction balanced premium simplicity with an energetic direct-to-consumer voice across desktop and mobile.
