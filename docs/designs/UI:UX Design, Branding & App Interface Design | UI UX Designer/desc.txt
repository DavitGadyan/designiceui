Fintech Brand Redesign – UI/UX Design, Branding & App Interface for Aurbitrage 💰📊

As a UI UX Designer, I led a complete rebranding and digital redesign for Aurbitrage, a fintech company offering smart arbitrage tools for crypto and financial markets. The challenge was to elevate the visual identity and usability of both their web app and marketing website, making the brand more modern, trustworthy, and conversion-driven.

➡️ Process:

Brand Identity & Design System:
We started with a strategic rebranding phase, where I introduced a refreshed visual identity – including a new color palette, typography, iconography, and a scalable design system. The goal was to give Aurbitrage a confident, clean, and tech-savvy appearance while improving UI consistency across all touchpoints.

Landing Page Redesign:
The landing page was completely restructured to enhance clarity and drive conversions. I proposed a new content architecture and designed high-impact UI sections with compelling visual hierarchy, product storytelling, and call-to-action zones that speak to both institutional and retail users.

Web App UI/UX Redesign:
The application's interface was overhauled with usability in mind. I improved dashboard layouts, table views, and user interaction patterns based on best UX practices for data-heavy fintech tools. The redesign ensures that both beginner and advanced users can easily analyze market data, execute trades, and monitor performance.

Responsive & Component-Based Design:
Every component was designed to be modular and responsive, ensuring smooth scalability across desktop and mobile environments. I prepared developer-ready assets, specifications, and guided implementation through detailed documentation.

🎯 Results:
Aurbitrage emerged with a bold new brand identity, a modern marketing site, and a clean, functional app interface. The redesign helped build user trust, clarify product offerings, and create a more competitive digital presence in the fintech space.
