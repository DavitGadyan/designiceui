My role. UX/UI Designer

Project description.

I designed a clinical dashboard for Northwell Health in Figma, building a clean, intuitive interface tailored for medical professionals. The design features clear data visualizations, accessible layout, and interactive filters that prioritize patient metrics. I also established UI components and responsive grid systems to ensure usability across devices while adhering to healthcare data best practices.

As the lead designer, I crafted a clinical dashboard from the ground up in Figma no preexisting template. My mission was to make complex patient data accessible, actionable, and trustworthy for healthcare professionals, while ensuring compliance and usability across devices.

Northwell Health needed a dashboard that could present real-time patient metrics in a clean, digestible format. The challenge was balancing clarity with complexity distilling dense data into visuals that inform critical decisions without overwhelming users.

Iterated low-to-high fidelity prototypes with interactive filters and hover info.

Included real-world data simulations for context (e.g., patient volume, avg. wait times).

Added explanatory tooltips to minimize misinterpretation and enhance clarity.

A scalable design system laid the foundation for future Northwell Health dashboard extensions (e.g., operational KPIs, financial metrics).
