Wealthcore is a private-banking landing page that reads as expensive by being quiet.
Nothing bounces. Every transition is a fade and a small rise.

Hero. Split screen, 55/45. Left holds a serif headline at clamp(2.75rem, 5.5vw, 4.75rem) with a
one-sentence subhead in muted grey and a single gold-bordered CTA. Right holds a floating account
card rendered in dark glass with a 1px #C8A96A33 border, tilting up to 6 degrees toward the
cursor with a spring, and a live-looking balance figure that ticks by small amounts every few
seconds. The tilt is the only playful thing on the page and that restraint is what sells it.

Trust bar. Six client wordmarks at 40% opacity, greyscale, lifting to 100% on hover.

Bento block. A 4-column asymmetric grid: one wide card holding a line chart with an animated
draw-on path, two square cards for security and custody, one tall card with a portrait-format
photograph of a hand and a document, warm-lit. Cards sit on #141416 with 20px radius and no
shadow at all - separation comes from the border and the background delta only.

Numbers. Assets under management, average tenure, jurisdictions. Set in the serif at large size
with the label beneath in 11px uppercase, 0.14em tracking.

Palette is off-black #0A0A0B, a single champagne gold #C8A96A for accents and rules, and a warm
off-white #E8E6E1 for text. Gold never fills a large area; it appears as hairlines, small caps
labels and one button border. Type pairs a high-contrast display serif with a neutral grotesque.

Use for private banking, wealth platforms, family offices, insurance, legal and anything where
the buyer needs to feel that the brand is older and steadier than they are.
