
My role. Senior UX/UI Designer, Web Design Lead, Product Design and E-commerce UX

Project description.

Austrian Audio needed a web design that matched the precision and prestige of their high-end audio hardware. The existing product pages lacked visual hierarchy and failed to communicate technical specs in a way that converted professional buyers. I redesigned the full web design and product UX in Figma, restructuring information hierarchy, building a responsive component system, and creating a product page design that balanced technical detail with clean UI. The new web design improved content scannability and delivered a consistent experience.

The challenge with premium hardware e-commerce is that the audience is technically sophisticated and skeptical. Generic product page web design templates fail because they prioritize aesthetics over specification clarity. My UX design process started with a competitive audit of leading pro-audio brands and a heuristic review of the existing site to identify where users were losing context or trust. From that research I built a revised UX architecture in Figma, separating product detail, use case, and technical specification into distinct page zones. The UI design used a restrained visual language that let product photography and hardware detail carry the brand weight. Component-level design decisions in Figma were documented for developer handoff with responsive breakpoint specs for mobile web. The result was a web design system that Austrian Audio could extend across new product lines without losing visual consistency or UX logic.
