My role. Lead designer, Senior UX/UI designer

Project description.

The Wame system allows football managers to track and analyze the results of a single player in a team and the team's performance. Based on this data, a football manager can improve the team's play. In addition to analytical answers, the system provides reports on the team's tournament position, allows you to compare the performance of different football leagues and much more.
