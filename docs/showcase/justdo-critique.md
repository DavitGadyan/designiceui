## Must fix
1. **`Link asChild` drops every function-style on its Pressable - week cards and the Week row lose their layout** - Reference: image_original6 (left) / image_original3 (right) - each session is a #1F1F1F card, ~24px radius, ~16px padding, ~120px tall, first one tinted; image_original4 - "Week 1" and ">" share one line. Build: `verify/critique/week/mobile-full.png` - rows are 350x80 with no fill, no padding, no tint, chips flush under the disc; `verify/critique/index/mobile-full.png` - the chevron renders on its own line under "Week 1" (link box 350x48). Cause: expo-router's `Link asChild` merges its own `style` with the child's, so a function `style` becomes `[undefined, fn]` and is discarded (`styles.row`, the `${color}14` tint, `styles.weekRow` never apply; the Button inside Link survives only because its `style` prop is a plain object). Fix: `app/week.tsx` lines 40-56 and `app/(tabs)/index.tsx` lines 72-77 - drop `Link asChild`; `useRouter()` (already imported in week.tsx) and `<Pressable onPress={() => router.push({ pathname: "/session/[id]", params: { id: s.id } })} style={({ pressed }) => [...]}>` / `router.push("/week")`. If you must keep Link, move all visual styles to an inner `<View style={styles.row}>`.
2. **Skills wheel and calendar widgets are missing** - Reference: image_original2 bottom-left - radial skills wheel: 5 coloured sectors (one per skill) over ~10 concentric rings, smiley centre, -/+ stepper with 10 segments (6 lit); bottom-right - month sheet: grabber, "< September >", Mon-Sun header, 5-row grid of round day cells, ring on today, dots on session days (12, 17, 18), "Training Sessions" legend pill. LAYOUT.md lists both (#9, #10). Build: none of the 10 routes has either (no calendar or wheel in `app/` or `components/`). Fix: `components/SkillWheel.tsx` - `npx expo install react-native-svg`; 10 concentric `Circle` rings + 5 `Path` sectors filled from `theme.colors.skill`, smiley centre, stepper row (round - / 10 segments / round +); mount under the My Progress list in `app/(tabs)/progress.tsx` (Overview tab). `components/CalendarSheet.tsx` - `Modal transparent` bottom sheet with grabber, 7-column grid of 44px round cells, dot on session days, legend pill; open it from the "4 weeks 2 days" card in `app/(tabs)/index.tsx` (wrap that Card in a Pressable).
3. **Session Stats ring ignores its value** - Reference: image_original6 (right) - the 90% ring has a ~36 degree dark gap at the top-right, ~16px stroke. Build: `verify/critique/progress/mobile-tall.png` - the ring is #E10600 at all 24 sampled angles (0-345 degrees): a 100% ring next to a "90%" label. Cause: `components/ui/Ring.tsx` lines 20-27 paint all four border sides of both half-circles in `color`, so rotating them changes nothing. Fix: `components/ui/Ring.tsx` - once react-native-svg is in for the wheel, draw a hairline track `Circle` and an arc `Circle` with `strokeWidth={stroke} strokeDasharray={`${c * value / 100} ${c}`} strokeLinecap="round" transform="rotate(-90)"`; if you keep Views, each rotating circle must be a half-ring (`borderLeftColor` and `borderBottomColor: "transparent"`), not a full one.

## Should fix
4. **Onboarding "Next" is not docked to the bottom** - Reference: image_original2 - on both onboarding phones the white Next pill sits at the bottom edge (pill bottom ~4% above the screen edge). Build: `verify/critique/onboarding-event/mobile-full.png` - Next spans y 568-624 of 844 with 220px of empty black below; `onboarding-goal` - y 653-709, 135px empty. Fix: `components/Screen.tsx` `content: { flexGrow: 1, paddingBottom: theme.spacing.xl }`; `app/onboarding/event.tsx` and `goal.tsx` - wrap the Button in `<View style={{ marginTop: "auto", paddingTop: 24, paddingBottom: insets.bottom }}>` with `useSafeAreaInsets()`; this also gives stack screens the bottom inset that `Screen` (edges top/left/right) currently leaves to iOS-only `contentInsetAdjustmentBehavior`.
5. **Session Stats is an inline card, the reference is a bottom sheet** - Reference: image_original6 (right) - sheet with grabber and ~28px top corners rising from ~45% of the screen over a dimmed page; ring ~62% of screen width; pager row at the sheet's foot. Build: `verify/critique/progress/mobile-tall.png` - a Card at y 801-1180 of a 1260px scroll, sixth block in the column; ring 180px (46% of width); no grabber, no scrim. Fix: `app/(tabs)/progress.tsx` - move the stats block into a `Modal transparent animationType="slide"` sheet (`backgroundColor: theme.colors.surface`, `borderTopLeftRadius/borderTopRightRadius: 28`, 36x4 grabber, scrim `rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`), opened by tapping a My Progress row; `<Ring size={240} stroke={16} />`.
6. **Progress tab strip is fixed, so the fourth tab is unreachable** - Reference: image_original5 (right) - Overview / Achievements / Emotions / Me... runs off the right edge as a horizontally scrolling strip. Build: `verify/critique/progress/mobile-full.png` + `measure.json` - "Mental" right edge = 409px on a 390px screen inside a plain flex row, so it can never scroll into view; each tab is 36px tall (< 44). Fix: `app/(tabs)/progress.tsx` lines 37-44 - wrap the tabs in `<ScrollView horizontal showsHorizontalScrollIndicator={false} contentContainerStyle={styles.tabs}>` (add `paddingRight: 20`), and `tab: { paddingBottom: 12, minHeight: 44, justifyContent: "flex-end" }`.
7. **Every heading is 700 Bold; the reference heads are regular/medium** - Reference: image_original1/5/6 - "My Plan", "Progress", "Goal Setting", "Mental strength rating" are regular-weight Satoshi; hierarchy comes from size (~40px vs 15px body), card titles are regular and single-line. Build: `measure.json` - 'My Plan' 34px Bold, 'Week 1' 22px Bold, every h3 Bold, because `components/ui/Text.tsx` line 25 forces `styles.bold` for display/h1/h2/h3; knock-on: "4 weeks 2 days" (18px Bold) wraps to 2 lines (45px tall) in the 129px card column. Fix: `app/_layout.tsx` load `PlusJakartaSans_500Medium`; `lib/theme.ts` add `fonts.displayMedium`; `Text.tsx` use it for display/h1/h2/h3 and keep 700 only when `bold` is passed; `app/(tabs)/index.tsx` `cardTitle` fontSize 17.
8. **Emotion chips are missing their radio** - Reference: image_original3 (bottom-right) - 9 chips in 2 columns, label left-aligned with a ~20px radio at the right end; selected = white border + filled radio. Build: `verify/critique/check-in/mobile-tall.png` - 9 chips, label centred, no radio. Fix: `components/ui/Chip.tsx` - add `radio?: boolean` rendering the 22px radio already styled in `check-in.tsx` (`radio/radioOn/radioDot`) after the label, with `flexDirection: "row", justifyContent: "space-between", paddingHorizontal: 18`; pass `radio` at `app/(tabs)/check-in.tsx` line 49.
9. **Session header is a flat slab, the reference is a full-bleed photograph** - Reference: image_original3 (left) - back, title and intro sit on a darkened portrait that fades to black above the info rows. Build: `verify/critique/session-1/mobile-tall.png` - #141414 block with 32px bottom corners (source comment: "gradient stand-in until an asset exists"). Fix: `app/session/[id].tsx` - `<ImageBackground source={require("@/assets/session-hero.jpg")} style={styles.hero} imageStyle={{ opacity: 0.55 }}>` with a bottom fade View to `theme.colors.ground`; generate the portrait with the higgsfield-imagery skill from DESIGN.md's "section-support" prompt (4:3, one athlete, dark, controlled contrast); give the image explicit width/height.
10. **Rating slider cannot be dragged and its thumb has no glow** - Reference: image_original5 (left) - continuous slider, ~36px glowing thumb the user drags. Build: `verify/critique/onboarding-rating/mobile-full.png` + `measure.json` - 40 invisible tap stops of 21x44px (`role=adjustable`), no pan handling; thumb 22px; the `shadow*` glow does not render on web. Fix: `components/ui/RatingSlider.tsx` - `onLayout` to capture track x/width, a `PanResponder` on `styles.track` with `onPanResponderMove: (_, g) => onChange(clamp(Math.round((g.moveX - trackX) / trackW * 10), 1, 10))`, keep the tap stops, thumb 32px, glow via `boxShadow: \`0 0 14px ${color}\`` (RN 0.76+).

## Polish
11. **Tab shell tells** (`app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx`) - (a) all five tab routes log "Minified React error #418" (hydration mismatch) on load while every stack route is clean, so the mismatch lives in the Tabs shell (custom `PlusDisc` icon / null label, or the `height`/`paddingTop` overrides differing between static render and client); read the full message with `npx expo export --platform web --no-minify`, and gate the custom icon behind a mounted flag if that is the source. (b) 1px #313131 hairline above the bar (`verify/critique/index/mobile-full.png`, y=756) where image_original1/4 show a borderless black bar - `tabBarStyle.borderTopWidth: 0`.
12. **Template partner claim leaks into JustDo copy** - "HYROX London 2026" (`app/(tabs)/index.tsx` line 35) and "Official mental training partner of" + NIKE / adidas / UNDER ARMOUR / HYROX / Red Bull... (`components/Brands.tsx` line 10, `app/(tabs)/more.tsx` line 25) are Getahead's own partnership claim from the reference description, now asserted for JustDo with real trademarks. Fix: a neutral event name ("City Marathon 2026"), strip title "Built with athletes from", placeholder wordmarks.

Inherited: 11px uppercase chip labels (AUDIO / 5 MINS / skill) and 13px goal-card body copy match the reference's own sizes - *inherited*, below the brief's 15px floor.

colours (accent #849CFF -> #E10600, accent2 -> #FF6B35, per-skill tints, violet button/ring/thumb fills now red), fonts (Satoshi -> Plus Jakarta Sans), logo, copy wording, product name, photography subject - not reviewed.

## Not verified
- Native safe-area insets, Stack/Tabs transitions and on-device pressed states (web probe only: Next opacity 1 -> 0.7 -> 1 on press).
- Where the reference intends the wheel and calendar to live; image_original2 shows them as widgets, not in a screen.
- `/session/[id]` pixels come from client-side navigation in the web export (no static HTML for the dynamic route); the 404 in the earlier `session-1` manifest was the direct-URL load.
- The browser's default orange focus outline on Tab (web export only) - not counted against a mobile target.
- Thumb glow and card shadows on native (web export drops `shadow*`).
